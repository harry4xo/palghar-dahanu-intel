Methodology

How facts get into the database and how summaries are calculated. No AI is used anywhere in this application: data comes from structured portals, keyword monitoring and researchers, and all summaries follow the fixed rules below.

One project, many sourced facts

Each project has one record and a unique ID (PDI-00001…). Everything we know about it — each stage change, investment figure, land record, approval or court order — is a separate fact with its own source, a short quote from that source, and a review status.

Conflicting information is kept side by side, never overwritten. A project's status, verification level and headline investment are calculated from its facts, so history is preserved.

Review

Facts enter as Pending review (from research or the monitoring inbox) and become Reviewed once a researcher has checked them against the source. The date and reviewer are recorded.

Facts copied field-for-field from MahaRERA by the crawler are marked reviewed automatically, because MahaRERA is the primary source.

Where a project has no reviewed stage facts yet, its stage is shown as provisional.

Source levels

  • Primary — government departments, regulators (MahaRERA, MoEFCC/PARIVESH, MIDC, NHAI, Railways), ministry notifications, court orders, company filings and official project documents.
  • Secondary — credible newspapers, business and industry publications, research reports.
  • Unverified / reported — local reports, property portals, unconfirmed announcements.

A project is corroborated when its facts come from at least two different publishers. Otherwise the page states that independent confirmation was not found.

Project lifecycle

Delayed, Stalled and Cancelled are flags shown alongside the stage; a later “Revived” entry clears Stalled or Cancelled. An announcement is never treated as approval, and an MoU never as an investment.

Investment figures

All figures are kept. When one number per project is needed (tables, totals), the headline is chosen by basis — sanctioned > official estimate > reported > MoU — then the most recent. Dashboard totals exclude MoU values (shown separately) exclude projects recorded as part of another project, so money isn't counted twice, and report separately any figure that covers a wider corridor than Palghar (e.g. the full 508 km bullet-train cost).

Funding has its own stages: Announced → Committed → Approved → Disbursed → Completed.

Micro-market activity

A dimension (RERA, developer, industrial, infrastructure, land, logistics, regulatory, funding) is evidenced when at least one fact dated within the period is backed by a primary or secondary source.

  • HIGH — three or more evidenced dimensions, and at least one project there has reached approval or beyond on a primary source.
  • MEDIUM — at least one evidenced dimension.
  • LOW — nothing evidenced. If a researcher logged a search that found nothing, the page says: “{{ no_material }}”

Duplicate matching

New documents are matched to existing projects by fixed rules, strongest first: same MahaRERA number; same name or recorded alternate name; same name after spelling normalisation (Vadhvan = Vadhavan = Wadhwan); similar name; shared survey numbers in the same village. Only the first is treated as certain — everything else is a suggestion for the researcher.

Locations

Village and town coordinates are place centroids from OpenStreetMap. Projects placed by village or taluka show as hollow markers on the map; only projects with a stated site location show as solid markers.

Linked projects (e.g. a logistics park driven by the port) are recorded only when a source states the relationship, never because two projects are close together.
